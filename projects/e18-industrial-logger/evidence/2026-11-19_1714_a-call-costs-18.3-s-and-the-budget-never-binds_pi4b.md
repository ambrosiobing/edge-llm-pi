## A CORRECTION TO MY OWN REASONING FOR RAISING THE BUDGET

I raised both defaults from 64 to 128 today, and gave two reasons. The second one, that the
four rows must be compared at one budget, stands. **The first one does not apply to this
prompt.** I argued that 64 truncates, from e04's measurement that a summary of this character
class needs 95 to 97 tokens. The answer this bench prompt produces is

    { "decision": "NOMINAL", "summary": "register 0 level 577 counts, span 141 counts over
      30 frames, temperature 42.4 C, pressure 5.8 bar." }

**138 bytes, a complete JSON object.** The grammar ends it, not the ceiling, and the figure
confirms it: the in-process arm read 18.307 s at a budget of 64 on Friday 13 November 2026
and reads 18.320 s at 128 today, a difference of 13 ms. **The budget never binds on this
prompt, so raising it changed the comparability of the four rows and not one millisecond of
their timing.** The fix was right and my stated reason for it was half wrong, which is worth
recording because the wrong half is the one that would have predicted a 50 per cent rise.

The FFI run followed the abi_test run, so its 462.96 MiB were already in the page cache and

against a hundred seconds cold.

## WHAT THIS DECIDES ABOUT THE QUALIFYING SITTING

The chapter's thermal fact: this board reaches its 80 C soft limit **23 minutes** after a
reboot under load, measured Friday 13 November 2026, and clause three discards any
**repetition** with a throttle bit set rather than the whole sitting. So the qualifying
window is about 1380 s.

    one cold load, for the daemon                       about  50 s
    three repetitions, warm loads, two plus one per      about  54 s
      repetition for abi_test and the FFI arm
    remaining for calls                                 about 1276 s
    at 18.3 s per call                                        69 calls

**The chapter's stated shape of 20 calls per arm repeated three times is 240 calls, which is
73 minutes and does not fit.** It was written on Friday 13 November 2026 against a figure
from a different design and was never checked against the thermal window. At four arms and
three repetitions the window admits **five calls per arm per repetition**: 60 calls, 1098 s,
plus about 104 s of loads, so roughly 20 minutes with three minutes of margin.

Five calls give a median over five and three repetitions give the run-to-run spread, which is
what this project has twice found to be the figure that matters: on Friday 13 November 2026
the within-run standard error understated the real spread by two orders of magnitude.
